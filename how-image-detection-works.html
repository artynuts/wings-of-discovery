🤖 What is Image Detection?

Our AI system analyzes images to identify archaeological artifacts. It was trained on thousands of artifact examples to recognize patterns and provide confidence scores.

⚙️ How It Works

  1. Upload images
  2. AI analyzes them
  3. Get results with confidence scores

💻 Technology Used

  • TensorFlow.js: Runs the AI model in your browser
  • Google Teachable Machine: Created the detection model
  • Neural Networks: Powers the pattern recognition

📊 Confidence Scores

  • 80-100%: High confidence
  • 50-80%: Medium confidence
  • 0-50%: Low confidence

Always verify results with an expert.

💡 Tips for Best Results

  • Use clear, well-lit images
  • Simple backgrounds work better
  • Make sure artifacts are in focus
  • Verify results with an expert

🔒 Privacy

Your images stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded or stored on servers.
