What is Image Detection?

Image detection uses artificial intelligence to automatically identify and classify objects in images. Our system is trained to recognize archaeological artifacts by learning patterns from thousands of labeled examples.

How It Works

  1. Upload: You provide images for analysis
  2. Process: Images are prepared and standardized
  3. Analyze: The AI model examines the images
  4. Score: The model assigns confidence scores to each prediction
  5. Display: Results show what artifacts were detected and how confident the model is

Understanding Confidence Scores

  • 80-100%: Very confident - generally reliable
  • 50-80%: Moderate confidence - review with an expert
  • 0-50%: Low confidence - needs expert verification

Important: AI detection is a tool to assist researchers. Always validate results with expert judgment.

Technology Stack

  • TensorFlow.js: Runs AI models in your browser
  • Google Teachable Machine: Trained the detection model
  • Neural Networks: Pattern recognition algorithms

What Affects Accuracy

  • Lighting: Good, consistent lighting helps
  • Angle: Photos from different angles are better
  • Background: Simple backgrounds work better
  • Image Quality: Clear, sharp images are more reliable

Privacy

Your images stay on your device. They are never uploaded to servers or stored. All analysis happens locally in your browser.

Limitations

  • Can only recognize artifacts it was trained on
  • Performance varies with image quality
  • Cannot replace expert archaeological analysis
  • Works best with clear, well-photographed artifacts
